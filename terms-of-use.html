03What Tells is — and what it is not

The app looks at one photograph of two people and describes what their bodies are doing in that frame: how close they're standing, which way they're turned, whether there's contact and whether it's returned, posture, expression. It turns that into a score and a write-up.

All of this happens on your device, using your phone's own vision capabilities. The app has no servers and needs no internet connection to work. Your photo is never sent anywhere — see the Privacy Policy.

The signals the app uses are drawn from published work on nonverbal behaviour. Applying them to a single still image is nonetheless inference, not measurement, and it is not a validated method for determining what anybody thinks or feels. The app tells you this on every result screen, and we mean it here too.

  • Not a basis for consequential decisions. Do not use it to decide anything affecting someone's relationships, safety, employment, housing, reputation or wellbeing.
